# ⚛️ Quantum for Everyone

Quantum mechanics explained in plain English, with playful interactive demos built in Vue.js.

**Live page:** https://jtwebguy.github.io/quantum-for-everyone/

## What's inside
- 🌊 Wave–particle duality — a double-slit simulator with a "peek" detector
- 🪙 Superposition & measurement — the quantum coin
- 🎯 Uncertainty principle — squeeze position, watch momentum spread
- 🧱 Quantum tunneling — throw balls at a wall
- 🧦 Entanglement — spooky linked socks
- 💡 Real-world uses and a quick quiz

Single `index.html`, Vue 3 from a CDN, no build step.
